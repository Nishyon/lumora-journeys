Since 2009

Made by peoplewho wander.

Lumora began with one belief: the best journeys are designed by people who know a place intimately — and care about you as much as the destination.

Our story

Fewer trips, far better ones.

We design around 1,200 journeys a year — deliberately. It means each traveller has a dedicated designer from first call to final transfer, and every detail gets the attention it deserves.

Our designers spend at least six weeks a year on the road, re-testing hotels, finding new guides and hunting for the quiet corners that make a journey feel like yours alone.

Clara WhitfieldFounder, Lumora Journeys
