Company overview

Built on expertise. Trusted for reliability.

Lumora Travel Group designs and delivers tailor-made luxury holidays, corporate travel programmes and global events. Our 320 specialists operate from London, Singapore and Cape Town, supported by a 24/7 operations centre.

Every journey is backed by full financial protection, rigorous supplier due diligence and a commitment to responsible travel that is independently audited each year.
